How do I save a photostrip?

On the final photostrip screen, tap Save to add your finished strip to your photo library. Make sure photo-saving permission is enabled if your device asks for it.

How do I share?

Tap Share on the final screen and choose an available app or destination from your device's share sheet.

How do I restore Premium?

Open the Premium purchase screen and select Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple account associated with your existing purchase. Access depends on whether your subscription remains active.

How do I cancel?

On iPhone, open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, select Polariscene if present, and follow Apple's cancellation instructions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.
