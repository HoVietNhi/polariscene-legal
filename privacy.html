Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

This policy describes how Polariscene handles information when you use our photobooth application and contact support.

Photos and camera access

Polariscene requests camera permission so you can capture photobooth photos. In version 1.0 Alone Mode, photos are edited and assembled into photostrips on your device; we do not operate a cloud photo-storage service for this feature. The app saves a photostrip to your photo library only when you choose Save. If you choose Share, your device opens its sharing options, and information you share is handled by the service you select.

Purchases and subscriptions

Polariscene uses Apple's in-app purchase system and RevenueCat to provide and restore premium access. Purchase status and transaction information, an app user identifier, and device or operating-system information may be processed to maintain your subscription and restore entitlements. We do not receive your full payment card number from Apple. For more information, see RevenueCat's privacy policy and Apple's privacy policy.

Support inquiries

If you email support, we receive the email address and information you choose to include in your message. Please do not send private photos, payment card details, or other sensitive information unless they are necessary to resolve your issue. We use support messages to respond to your request.

Data retention and choices

Photos saved to your device remain under your control through your device's photo library. You can manage camera and photo access in device settings. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple account settings. Purchase records may be retained by Apple and RevenueCat under their own policies and applicable requirements. To ask about information we control, email us at polariscene.support@gmail.com.

Children

Polariscene is not designed to collect personal information knowingly from children under 13. If you believe a child has shared personal information with us through support, please contact us.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as app features change. The latest version will appear here with an updated date. Questions about privacy can be sent to polariscene.support@gmail.com.
