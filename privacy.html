Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Polariscene handles information when you use our photobooth application, visit our website, or contact our support team.

Photos and camera access

Polariscene requests camera permission so you can capture photobooth photos.

In version 1.0 Alone Mode, photos are edited and assembled into photostrips on your device. We do not operate a cloud photo-storage service for this feature.

Polariscene saves a photostrip to your photo library only when you choose Save.

If you choose Share, your device opens its sharing options. Information you share is handled by the service you select.

Purchases and subscriptions

Polariscene uses Apple's in-app purchase system and RevenueCat to provide and restore premium access.

Purchase status, transaction information, an app user identifier, and device or operating-system information may be processed to maintain your subscription and restore entitlements.

We do not receive your full payment card number from Apple.

For more information, please see RevenueCat's Privacy Policy and Apple's Privacy Policy .

Support requests

When you contact Polariscene through our support form, we collect the email address, support topic, and message you choose to provide.

We use this information to understand your request, respond to your questions, and provide customer support.

Our website uses Formspree, a third-party form service, to process support requests. Information submitted through the form is transmitted to and stored by Formspree and may be forwarded to our support email.

If you contact us directly by email, we receive your email address and the information you include in your message.

Please do not include passwords, private photos, full payment card details, or other sensitive information in your support request.

Data retention and your choices

Photos saved to your device remain under your control through your device's photo library.

You can manage camera and photo access in your device settings.

You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple account settings.

Purchase records may be retained by Apple and RevenueCat under their own policies and applicable requirements.

Support requests may remain in our support inbox and Formspree account as needed to respond to inquiries and maintain support records.

To ask about personal information we control, including a request to delete a support message, contact us at polariscene.support@gmail.com .

Children

Polariscene is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe a child has shared personal information with us through support, please contact us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Polariscene and its features change.

The latest version will appear on this page with an updated date.

Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how Polariscene handles information, please contact us at polariscene.support@gmail.com .

You can also visit our Support page .
