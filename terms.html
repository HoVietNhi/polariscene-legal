Last updated: September 21, 2026

Your use of Polariscene is subject to Apple's Standard End User License Agreement (EULA), unless different terms are provided when you install the app.

Polariscene Starlight

Polariscene offers optional auto-renewable monthly and yearly subscriptions that unlock premium photostrip frames. The app displays the applicable price and billing period before purchase. Availability and prices may vary by region.

Renewal and cancellation

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled according to Apple's subscription terms. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple account settings. Removing the app does not automatically cancel it.

Restore and support

Use Restore Purchases in the app to recover an eligible active subscription. For purchase support, email polariscene.support@gmail.com. Apple handles billing and refund requests according to its policies.
